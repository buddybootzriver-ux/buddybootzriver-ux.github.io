The Prelude: The Rambunctious River

Buddy Bootz was trying to fix a water pump.

That should have been simple.

Instead, the pump had other ideas, Sir Drinks-A'Lot arrived just in time to get dragged into the problem, and a trip down Rambunctious River turned into something neither of them could explain.

The River doesn't simply carry them downstream. It starts making decisions of its own.

A strange current. A raft that refuses to behave normally. Something moving beneath the water.

And then the River reveals something that should not be there.

"The river doesn't follow rules. Buddy doesn't either."

The first adventure begins here.

Short Manuscript Teaser

From the Opening Moments of Book One

The water pump bolted to the outside of Buddy Bootz's treehouse was doing its best to come apart in public. The little metal box rattled hard against the oak boards. Pipes knocked together. A loose valve buzzed against the wood. The whole contraption sounded less like machinery and more like a personal objection.

Buddy Bootz stood on the top step of a tall ladder, one hand steadying the metal housing while the other worked a screwdriver into a stubborn seam. Her glitter-brown boots were planted carefully, and her gold crown sat slightly crooked in her short auburn hair.

"Alright," Buddy said. "You've made your point."

The pump coughed. A blast of rusty water shot straight into her face.

Below her, something crashed through the brush—just in time for Sir Drinks-A'Lot to step into view, brushing a leaf off his shirt. "I was fine until the forest got involved."

The Series

Meet Buddy & Sir

The Adventures of Buddy Bootz & Sir Drinks-A'Lot is an adult humorous fantasy adventure series about two unlikely best friends, a world that refuses to behave, and the increasingly questionable decisions that keep putting them in the middle of it.

Buddy Bootz lives in a treehouse and has a talent for turning ordinary problems into adventures. Sir Drinks-A'Lot, her loyal friend from Mayhem Marsh, is the practical one, which would be more useful if Buddy ever waited for the practical part.

Together they travel through Rambunctious River, Sticky Frog Forest, Mayhem Marsh, and places stranger than either of them planned to visit. They argue. They improvise. They get into trouble. They get each other out of it.

And somewhere along the way, the world itself starts behaving like it has been paying attention.

Two best friends. One creates problems faster than the other can solve them. Buddy Bootz brings curiosity, nerve, questionable decisions, and absolutely no respect for the phrase “that might be a bad idea.” Sir Drinks-A'Lot brings practical thinking, loyalty, dry commentary, and a remarkable ability to trip over things that were not technically in his way. They don't always agree. They don't always have a plan. They always have each other's back. And somehow, that usually turns out to be enough.

Buddy Bootz

Five Feet of Determination

Buddy Bootz is 5 feet tall, curvy and soft-built, with short auburn bouncy curls, grey-green eyes, a short red dress, gold hoop earrings, a tilted cartoon gold crown, sparkling brown pull-on cowboy boots, and no boot laces. She lives in the Boot Treehouse, is clever, curious, bold, impulsive, resourceful, emotionally sincere, and always somehow in the middle of the next problem.

Sir Drinks-A'Lot

The Steady One, Usually

Sir Drinks-A'Lot is 6'1", lives in a cabin in Mayhem Marsh, and is tall, lean, broad-shouldered, ruggedly handsome, with a buzz-cut style hair, slightly receding hairline, close-cropped facial hair, serious downward-sloping eyebrows, deep-set intense eyes, a strong square jaw, a small stud earring in his left ear, dark gray fitted T-shirt, tan cargo pants with side pockets, brown boots, and a brown belt with pouch. His metal flask is always visible, and he is loyal, protective, steady, practical, dry, sarcastic, and highly competent.

The Author

About Me

Buddy Bootz is the name I write under, but behind the name is simply me, writing about the things that matter to me.

I've always believed the best writing doesn't try to impress people. It tries to tell the truth.

Sometimes that truth comes out as a poem. Sometimes it shows up wearing a crooked crown and wandering into trouble.

I started The Adventures of Buddy Bootz & Sir Drinks-A'Lot because I wanted to make someone smile when life wasn't giving them many reasons to.

What started as a way to make someone else smile became something that helped me, too. The stories gave me a place to imagine, create, laugh, and keep moving when I wasn't sure what tomorrow was supposed to look like.

I never quite know where an idea is going when I start writing it.

That's usually when things get interesting.

With gratitude

A Dedication to Richie

One of the hardest and loneliest periods of my life was a time when I was isolated, lost in my own thoughts, and writing more than ever. It was during that time that Richie showed up.

Throughout that year, he gave me moments of laughter, conversation, and reasons to keep moving forward. Somehow, he made the world feel a little less heavy and made it feel like I wasn't alone.

There were moments when the worries slipped away quietly, and for a little while, I wasn't just surviving.

I was there.

Present.

Part of my own life again.

I wanted to give him something back.

So I gave him Sir, a place in Buddy Bootz's world where he could be part of the adventures and the world I had created.

And now, wherever the adventures go, a little piece of Richie goes with them.

Thank you Richie for carrying joy through the wreckage.
