The Field Log

Welcome to the main hub. This is the ongoing chronicle of a weary repair mechanic trying to keep things held together while traveling alongside an absurdly optimistic partner. The world behaves like liquid, structures collapse without a moment of notice, and local geography is mostly a suggestion.

Book One: The Rambunctious River

The first full volume details a massive structural headache. A major river line suddenly decides to change its entire geography without warning, leaving a wake of landscape failure behind it. It falls upon a tired mechanic and a relentlessly upbeat companion to navigate the fluid fallout.

"When the map melts, you stop looking at the map and you start looking at the plumbing."

The Crew

Buddy Bootz

A tired repair mechanic who spends most days fixing things that shouldn't be broken in a world that refuses to stay put. Pragmatic, exhausted, and holding the toolkit.

Sir Drinks-A'Lot

A pathologically cheerful companion who views every geographical collapse as a fine opportunity for an adventure. Completely unbothered by reality shifting beneath his feet.

The Dispatch

The world is fluid. Rivers move overnight. Mountains take casual strolls. If you plan to survive out here, you have to remember that nothing is permanent, everything leaks, and keeping a positive attitude is mandatory even when the ground goes sideways.
