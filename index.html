The Plan is the Problem

There are stories where everything goes according to plan. This is not one of them. Buddy Bootz lives in a treehouse above a river that behaves like it has opinions. Sir Drinks-A’Lot comes from the marsh with just enough sense to survive her ideas and just enough loyalty to follow them anyway. Together, they build plans that collapse beautifully. Rafts drift where they shouldn’t. Maps don’t stay honest. And somewhere between bad decisions and better intentions, something real begins to form. This is a whimsical fantasy adventure series built on humor, movement, and character-driven storytelling.

About the Author

Buddy Bootz is a pen name. The person behind it is a writer.

Most of what she writes lives somewhere between humor and something heavier — because that's where real things tend to live. Things don't stay one thing for long, and neither do her stories. The chaos is real. So is the loyalty underneath it.

She started writing as a way through difficult moments. It wasn't planned, wasn't structured. It turned into something she didn't want to let go of.

The Adventures of Buddy Bootz & Sir Drinks-A'Lot came out of that — the same mix of mess and meaning, told through a different lens.

She's still writing. Still building. Still figuring it out as she goes, but with intention now.
