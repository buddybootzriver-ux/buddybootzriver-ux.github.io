ENTER THE RIVER MEET BUDDY & SIR

WHAT THIS STORY IS

This is an adult fantasy comedy series following Buddy Bootz and Sir Drinks-A-Lot as they travel the Rambunctious River, where every stop becomes a problem that should not legally exist.

It is not a hero’s journey. It is a “why is this happening again” journey.

Expect chaotic adventures, emotional humor, absurd magical geography, and consequences that always feel personal.

THE PEOPLE MAKING EVERYTHING WORSE (INTENTIONALLY OR NOT)

Buddy Bootz

Buddy Bootz is a chaos-driven instigator who turns small problems into large problems and calls it progress.

Strengths: fearless decisions, emotional honesty, accidental leadership

Weaknesses: planning, restraint, consequences

Sir Drinks-A’Lot

Sir Drinks-A’Lot is a grounded companion with dry humor and the patience of someone who has survived too much.

Strengths: logic, stability, sarcasm

Weaknesses: Buddy Bootz, exhaustion, sarcasm as coping mechanism

THE RAMBUNCTIOUS RIVER AND ITS BAD INFLUENCE

The Rambunctious River is not a setting. It is a participant.

It changes direction, interrupts conversations, and occasionally decides geography is optional.

Along its edges are: Sticky Frog Forest, Lantern Dock, and Mayhem Marsh.

Nothing stays consistent here. Not even the rules.

WHAT IT SOUNDS LIKE INSIDE THIS WORLD

Buddy said it was a simple plan. Sir did not respond immediately, which was his version of screaming.

The river moved faster, like it already knew this was going to be funny.

It usually was. For everyone except Sir.
