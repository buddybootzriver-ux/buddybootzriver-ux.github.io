A First Look at The Rambunctious River

Buddy Bootz solved most problems the way other people stepped over puddles: by putting one boot squarely in the middle of them.

The town pump had stopped pumping, which was the sort of crisis that made a person look important without necessarily making them useful.

"It is, technically, still a pump," Sir Drinks-A'Lot said, peering at it with the grave expression of a man who had already seen a worse day before breakfast.

"Then why is the handle smoking?" Buddy asked.

"Because," he said, "the handle appears to be fighting the water."
