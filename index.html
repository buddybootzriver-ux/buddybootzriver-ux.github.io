Two Contrasting Souls. One Breaking World.

The Adventures of Buddy Bootz & Sir Drinks-A'Lot is a series of standalone books built on witty observations, bad decisions, and the emotional truth of a world that doesn't sit still.

Buddy Bootz

Bold. Impulsive. Confident Chaos.

A glamorous disaster with a gold crown and a frequent habit of turning small problems into much larger ones. Buddy operates on the firm belief that if you move fast enough, the laws of cause and effect might simply lose track of you.

Sir Drinks-A'Lot

Loyal. Practical. Dependable.

A courageous strategist forced to survive Buddy's solutions. With a steady wrench and a full flask, he provides the logic and the valiant guardianship required to survive her momentum.

About the Author

Buddy Bootz writes stories and poems that find wonder in ordinary things and aren't afraid to smile at the strange corners of life. Drawn to contrasts—humor beside heartbreak, chaos beside kindness—her work is an honest reflection of lived experience and memory.

"If my writing stays with you a little longer than you expected, then I've done exactly what I set out to do."
