A Humorous Fantasy Book Series

Welcome to the official, unvarnished chronicling of two companions dealing with light infrastructure breakdown, shifting local borders, and a world that outright refuses to stay still.

Buddy Bootz wants to fix the immediate structural problems.

Sir Drinks-A’Lot wants to assume everything is operating on schedule.

The landscape itself continues to react unpredictably to their conversations.

The Emotional Center of the Riverbank

The Practical Grounding

Buddy Bootz

Driven by stubborn optimism, heart, and the complicated desire to make sure the things around him stop breaking down. He is the logic trying to organize a messy world.

The Absolute Certainty

Sir Drinks-A'Lot

Armed with unbreakable confidence, a steady flask, and a serene inner conviction that unexpected complications are simply how local geography shows its personality.

A Setting with Its Own Intentions

Buddy's Treehouse

Built high enough to keep an eye on the shifting meadows, this retreat serves as the primary processing center for Buddy’s notes, sketches, and repair ideas.

It has the distinct advantage of staying in the same coordinate system most days.

Sir's Place & The Cabin

A structure that reflects its owner: surprisingly sturdy, entirely casual, and positioned in a spot where the wind makes a pleasant whistling noise through the roof tiles.

The Rambunctious River & Water Pump

The main artery of local trouble. It flows backwards when it gets bored, and it interacts directly with an overly complex iron water pump that requires regular maintenance and a high degree of patience.

The Map Prelude

The active record of where things used to be. It is less of an authoritative reference tool and more of a conversation starter, given that boundaries settle like loose liquid.
