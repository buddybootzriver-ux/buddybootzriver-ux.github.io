I'm Buddy Bootz.

I write stories, poems, and the occasional wonderfully bad idea that seems perfectly reasonable right up until the moment it isn't.

My stories don't sit in a neat line. They wander. They argue with themselves. They get funny, then quiet, then honest when nobody asked them to.

I lean into humor hard. Not the polished kind. The kind that slips in sideways when things are getting too heavy to hold straight. The kind that laughs a little too loudly in the wrong moment because silence would mean something else entirely.

As a writer, I'm drawn to contrasts. Humor beside heartbreak. Wonder beside reality. Chaos beside kindness. I think that's where some of the most interesting stories live.

And then there are my poems. Some are raw and emotional. Some are playful and absurd. Both are real. Both are mine.
