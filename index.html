Hello. I’m Buddy Bootz. If you were expecting a grand, majestic fantasy chronicle where a chosen hero fulfills an ancient prophecy, I am afraid you have wandered onto the wrong riverbank.

Mostly, this is an official account of my friend Sir Drinks-A'Lot and me trying to handle light infrastructure breakdown, uncooperative plumbing, and a local map that changes its layout when we aren't looking.

Book One: The Rambunctious River

Our debut adventure focuses entirely on what happens when a perfectly pleasant afternoon stroll turns into an infrastructure emergency. The river decides to completely ignore its traditional boundaries, a mechanical pump starts exhibiting an attitude, and Sir Drinks-A'Lot manages to lose our lunch rations within twenty minutes.

This is a polished, fully illustrated book that skips the heavy-handed chosen-one tropes and gets straight to the good stuff: snappy banter, escalating logic, and two friends stubborn enough to survive an ecosystem that answers back.

"We aren't lost, Buddy," Sir said, chest puffed out proudly as the river began flowing sideways. "We are simply expanding our geographic horizons."

The Core Dynamic

Two Friends Against Highly Irregular Geography

We didn’t exactly ask to be the center of an entire book series, yet here we are. Sir Drinks-A'Lot treats our daily survival as a highly successful parade, while I am usually left holding the wrench.

The Reluctant Repairman

Buddy Bootz (That's Me)

I possess a notebook full of architectural schematics, a healthy respect for solid ground, and a stubborn belief that if I just tighten enough bolts, the world will eventually make sense. It hasn't happened yet, but I remain optimistic.

The Confident Navigator

Sir Drinks-A'Lot

Sir has never met a crisis he couldn’t turn into a comfortable rest stop. He travels with an unshakeable inner peace, a flask that never seems completely empty, and the serene certainty that getting lost is simply an adventure in italics.

The Ink-Stained Hands

Meet Buddy Bootz

When I am not actively securing my treehouse against sudden local wind storms or making sure Sir Drinks-A'Lot hasn't wandered off into a patch of mischievous brambles, I sit down with my inkpot and write down our adventures.

I wanted to create books that feel exactly like our corner of the woods: warm, slightly chaotic, heavy on visual details, and entirely driven by the comfort of good companionship. I mix the ink myself, draw the map lines by hand, and double-check every single page to ensure the narrative doesn't suddenly dissolve into a puddle of river water.

No corporate teams, no focus groups—just a clean, homemade story straight from my desk to your hands.

Tour of the Property

Places That Keep Me Awake At Night

My Treehouse

This is my structural operational headquarters. It is built high into the branches of a tree that, thankfully, has very deep roots. I keep my drafts, my spare tools, and my tea supply up here. It is one of the few places around here that stays exactly where I left it yesterday.

Sir's Place

Sir’s cabin is located just far enough down the lane to avoid my early-morning carpentry noises. It is an incredibly cozy space, mostly because he refuses to stress over minor details like a door that squeaks or windows that rattle when the valley wind decides to hum a tune.

The Rambunctious River & Water Pump

This is the source of my persistent headaches. The river changes direction whenever it feels unappreciated, and this iron water pump is my constant mechanical adversary. If you pull the handle too quickly, it coughs up old poems; if you pull it too slowly, the riverbanks start sliding into the grass.

The Map Prelude

We tried drawing a definitive map of our surroundings once. It was a disaster. The mountains kept sliding to the left to catch the evening sun, and the streams kept switching valleys. Consider this map a rough set of suggestions rather than actual, legal coordinates.

Live Logs

From the Riverbank

Welcome to our permanent update station. This is where I jot down direct notes about manuscript progress, ink studies, and messages that come in from readers across the water.

Volume One Typography Integration 92% Done

I am currently hand-checking the layout lines of *The Rambunctious River*. The print blocks are aligning neatly, ensuring the text panels don't crowd the margins when the book bindings are stitched down.

Volume Two Layout Outlining Structural Draft

The layout charts for our second book are pinned up in my treehouse. The dialogue sequences are clean, and I am locking down scenes where an uncooperative hedge maze keeps shifting its rows while Sir tries to fry sausages inside it.

Paper Absorption Experiments

Our heavy paper stock drinks ink very quickly on damp afternoons. I spent three days adjusting cross-hatching weights on the riverbank landscape drawings to make sure the shaded lines stay crisp instead of bleeding into a blurry blotch at the press.

The Geometry of Sir's Flask

Interesting detail: Sir’s leather flask changed shapes three times across my early sketches. I finally carved a small wooden model to keep on my desk so it stays uniform whether he is shaking it at an angry frog king or using it to brace a loose workbench leg.
