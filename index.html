The Plan is the Problem (A Character-Driven Fantasy Story)

There are stories where everything goes according to plan. This is not one of them. Buddy Bootz lives in a treehouse above a river that behaves like it has opinions. Sir Drinks-A’Lot comes from the marsh with just enough sense to survive her ideas and just enough loyalty to follow them anyway. Together, they build plans that collapse beautifully. Rafts drift where they shouldn’t. Maps don’t stay honest. And somewhere between bad decisions and better intentions, something real begins to form. This is a whimsical fantasy adventure series built on humor, movement, and character-driven storytelling.

What Makes This Different from Traditional Fantasy

Most fantasy asks: “What is the quest?” This one asks: “What happens when the people matter more than the plan?”

  • ✨ Character-driven storytelling over exposition
  • 🎭 Humor that escalates into chaos
  • ❤️ Emotional depth without slowing pacing
  • 🌊 A reactive, unpredictable world

The Rambunctious River

The Rambunctious River shifts, reacts, and reveals things when it wants to. Rafts carry bad ideas. Lanterns glow with intent. The further you go, the less certain anything becomes. Once you’re in it, you don’t control where it takes you.
