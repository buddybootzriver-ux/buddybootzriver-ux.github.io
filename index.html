Volume One Launch Profile

Book One: The Rambunctious River

SKU: BB-BK1-HC Fully Illustrated Hardcover Premium Edition 142 Pages of Panoramic Paper June 2026 Arrival

Our debut adventure focuses entirely on what happens when a perfectly pleasant afternoon stroll turns into an infrastructure emergency. The local river decides to completely ignore its traditional boundaries, a mechanical pump starts exhibiting an attitude, and Sir Drinks-A'Lot manages to lose our lunch rations within twenty minutes.

This is a polished, fully illustrated book that skips the heavy-handed chosen-one tropes and gets straight to the good stuff: snappy banter, escalating logic, and two friends stubborn enough to survive an ecosystem that answers back.

"We aren't lost, Buddy," Sir said, chest puffed out proudly as the river began flowing sideways. "We are simply expanding our geographic horizons."
