The Plan is the Problem

The town pump stopped pumping. Buddy Bootz decided this was her problem to fix. It was not her problem to fix.

Sir Drinks-A'Lot had a working plan, a wrench, and a deeply reasonable objection to everything that happened next. The river had other ideas.

Book One is a civic disaster in progress, two people who should not have left the dock, and a water supply that has decided to express itself.

"I am fixing it into a different problem."

Buddy Bootz solved most problems the way other people stepped over puddles: by putting one boot squarely in the middle of them.

“You’re not fixing it, Buddy,” Sir said, watching another bolt vanish into the froth of the Rambunctious River. “You’re just making the breaking more expensive.”

Buddy wiped a smear of axle grease across her forehead. “It’s a process, Sir. Progress is rarely tidy.”

“Progress involves a result,” Sir countered. “This is just enthusiastic disassembly.”

“I am fixing it into a different problem,” Buddy insisted, and then the river decided to join the conversation.

About the Author

Buddy Bootz is a pen name. The person behind it is a writer.

She started writing as a way through difficult moments. It wasn't planned, wasn't structured. It turned into something she didn't want to let go of.

Most of what she writes lives somewhere between humor and something heavier—because that's where real things tend to live.
