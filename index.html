Volume One Cycle

The Rambunctious River

The water pump bolted to the outside of Buddy Bootz's treehouse was doing its best to come apart in public. The little metal box attached to the side of the platform rattled violently as if the entire concept of plumbing had personally insulted it.

Pipes clanked. A loose valve vibrated. Buddy Bootz stood on the very top step of a tall ladder, working a screwdriver into a stubborn seam when the pump coughed and released a mechanical BURP—releasing an environmental landscape of pure, unhinged magic.
