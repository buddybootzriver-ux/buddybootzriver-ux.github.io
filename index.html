A river trip turns into a civic disaster in progress.

Buddy Bootz doesn't plan badly — she doesn't plan at all, which has the same result but more momentum.

Sir Drinks-A'Lot would prefer a responsible itinerary, functioning equipment, and a river that stays where rivers are supposed to stay.

Book One begins with a broken town pump and ends significantly wetter than it started.

Don’t take our word for it. Here’s where it starts.

"I am fixing it into a different problem."

Buddy Bootz solved most problems the way other people stepped over puddles: by putting one boot squarely in the middle of them.

The town pump had stopped pumping, which was the sort of crisis that made a person look important without necessarily making them useful.

"It is, technically, still a pump," Sir Drinks-A'Lot said, peering at it with the grave expression of a man who had already seen a worse day before breakfast.

"Then why is the handle smoking?" Buddy asked.

"Because," he said, "the handle appears to be fighting the water."

Meet the Chaos

Buddy Bootz

Fixes problems by making new ones—usually more interesting ones. She has a different relationship with consequences than most.

Sir Drinks-A'Lot

Competent, cautious, and constitutionally opposed to half-flooded town squares. He carries a wrench, a spoon, and a contingency prayer.

Where They Come Back To

Buddy’s Place

A treehouse built on the kind of decisions that should have been stopped halfway through—but weren’t.

It leans. It creaks. It somehow works anyway. And no matter what goes wrong out there, this is where she ends up standing like it all made sense.

Sir’s Place

Everything here has a place. Everything works. Nothing leaks unless it absolutely has to.

It is, by all reasonable standards, a safe environment—which is exactly why it never stays that way for long.

About the Author

Buddy Bootz is a pen name. The person behind it is a writer.

Most of what she writes lives somewhere between humor and something heavier—because that's where real things tend to live. Things don’t stay one thing for long, and neither do her stories. The chaos is real. So is the loyalty underneath it.

She started writing as a way through difficult moments. It wasn’t planned, wasn’t structured. It turned into something she didn’t want to let go of.

The Adventures of Buddy Bootz & Sir Drinks-A’Lot came out of that—the same mix of mess and meaning, told through a different lens.

She’s still writing. Still building. Still figuring it out as she goes, but with intention now.
