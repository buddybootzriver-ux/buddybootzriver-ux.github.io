The Prelude: The Rambunctious River

Buddy is repairing the water pump outside her treehouse when the machine objects by spraying her in the face. Sir arrives just in time to be recruited into the repair. Buddy solves the problem with what she calls “strategic whiskey,” the pump unexpectedly works, and a squirrel steals her screwdriver.

After the tools are put away, Buddy feels that something is waiting. She and Sir head down through the familiar forest to the Rambunctious River, climb onto their raft, and drift into what should have been an ordinary morning.

It isn't.

The river begins steering them. The current corrects their path. A loose board follows the same invisible channel. Gold light moves beneath the water. The river is no longer merely carrying them.

Then the water turns glass-smooth, stone rises, massive hedges unfold, and a maze builds itself in front of their raft. The river closes behind them and carries them toward the entrance.

“And this,” Buddy says, “is where the real adventure begins.”

The Series

Buddy & Sir

The stories are standalone adventures inside the same strange world, with Buddy and Sir returning to places that do not always agree to stay where they were left.

Buddy Bootz

Five Feet of Determination

Buddy lives in a treehouse, wears a tilted gold crown, a short red dress, gold hoop earrings, and glitter-brown boots. She is bold, reckless, emotionally sincere, and far too willing to test whether a terrible idea might work.

Sir Drinks-A'Lot

The Steady One, Usually

Sir lives nearby on Mayhem Marsh. He is loyal, dry, practical when Buddy isn't, clumsy when circumstances allow, and never seems to travel without his flask.

The Author

About Me

Buddy Bootz is the name I write under, but behind the name is simply me, writing about the things that matter to me.

I've always believed the best writing doesn't try to impress people. It tries to tell the truth.

Sometimes that truth comes out as a poem. Sometimes it shows up wearing a crooked crown and wandering into trouble.

I started The Adventures of Buddy Bootz & Sir Drinks-A-Lot because I wanted to make someone smile when life wasn't giving them many reasons to.

What started as a way to make someone else smile became something that helped me, too. The stories gave me a place to imagine, create, laugh, and keep moving when I wasn't sure what tomorrow was supposed to look like.

I never quite know where an idea is going when I start writing it.

That's usually when things get interesting.
