Meet the Duo

Buddy Bootz

Bold, impulsive, and frequently turning small problems into much larger ones. Buddy operates on the firm belief that if you move fast enough, the laws of cause and effect might simply lose track of you.

Sir Drinks-A'Lot

Loyal, practical, and frequently forced to survive Buddy's solutions. With a steady wrench and a full flask, he provides the logic and the valiant guardianship required to survive her momentum.
