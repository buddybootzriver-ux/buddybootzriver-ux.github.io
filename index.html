It starts small. A broken pump. A bad decision. A splash of whiskey where it definitely doesn’t belong. And then something shifts. The water listens. The current corrects. The quiet stops behaving like quiet. Buddy Bootz doesn’t follow rules. Sir Drinks-A’Lot tries to. It has never mattered. Because once the river decides where you’re going— you’re already on your way.

Begin with The Rambunctious River

The water pump bolted to the outside of Buddy Bootz’s treehouse was trying to come apart in public.

The little metal box rattled hard against the oak boards. Pipes knocked. A loose valve buzzed. Something inside snapped…

Buddy Bootz stood on the top step of a tall ladder, one hand steadying the metal housing while the other worked a screwdriver into a stubborn seam.

The pump coughed.

A blast of rusty water shot straight into her face.

Buddy went still.

Water dripped off her nose.

“…That was personal.”

And that was before the river changed direction.

Buddy Bootz

She fixes things that don’t want to be fixed. Names things that don’t agree. Wins arguments with objects. Red dress. Glitter-brown boots. Crown slightly crooked. Chaos doesn’t follow her. It adjusts around her.

Sir Drinks-A’Lot

He knows how things are supposed to work. This has not helped him. Steady hands. Sharp timing. Flask always accounted for. He doesn’t start trouble. He just happens to be standing next to it.

The Rambunctious River

It looks like water. It behaves like intention. It pulls. It corrects. It decides. And sometimes— it builds things that weren’t there a moment ago. If the surface goes still, you’re already too late.
