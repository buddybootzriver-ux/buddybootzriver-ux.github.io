Begin with The Rambunctious River

The water pump bolted to the outside of Buddy Bootz’s treehouse was doing its best to come apart in public.

The little metal box rattled hard against the oak boards. Pipes knocked. A loose valve buzzed. Something inside snapped…

Buddy Bootz stood on the top step of a tall ladder, one hand steadying the metal housing while the other worked a screwdriver into a stubborn seam.

The pump coughed.

A blast of rusty water shot straight into her face.

Buddy went still.

Water dripped off her nose.

“…That was personal.”

And that was before the river noticed them.

Meet Buddy Bootz

She doesn’t cause chaos.
That would suggest intention.
Chaos simply… arrives when she does.

Barefoot brave in spirit (never in boots), Buddy Bootz lives above the river in a treehouse that has seen things it refuses to explain.

She laughs first, questions later, and somehow always lands exactly where she’s needed… even if it’s not where she meant to be.

Meet Sir Drinks-A’Lot

If Buddy is the spark, Sir is the steady hand that keeps the world from catching fire.

He doesn’t lead her into chaos.
He’s the reason they make it back out.

A builder, a fixer, and an accidental hero more often than he’d prefer, Sir doesn’t chase chaos. He walks beside it.

And when things go wrong (they will), he’s the one who gets her back on her feet… usually while trying not to fall himself.
