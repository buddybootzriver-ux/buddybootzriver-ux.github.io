About the Author

A few notes from the observant, occasionally overconfident explorer who keeps a notebook close whenever the river starts behaving strangely.

The chronicler of the river

Buddy Bootz

Buddy Bootz is a curious maker, map scribbler, and devoted recorder of the world around the Rambunctious River. He is at his happiest with a fresh notebook, a dependable set of tools, and a plan that has not yet been tested by the local geography.

His stories follow the small moments that become big adventures: a water pump with a mind of its own, a path that refuses to stay put, or a peaceful afternoon that suddenly needs a raft, a lantern, and a little optimism.

When he is not sketching the next discovery, Buddy can be found repairing his treehouse, comparing maps with yesterday's version, and trying to convince the river that it has already made enough unexpected turns for one day.

Author's Note

I wanted this series to feel like opening a well-worn notebook after an interesting day outdoors: a little muddy around the edges, full of sketches, and packed with the kind of problems that are much more fun to solve with a friend nearby.

The Rambunctious River is a place for curious readers, brave tinkerers, thoughtful daydreamers, and anyone who has ever watched a perfectly reasonable plan become something delightfully unexpected. I hope these adventures leave you smiling, imagining, and ready to see where the next bend leads.

— Buddy Bootz
